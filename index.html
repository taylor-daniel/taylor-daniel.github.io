Case studies

Digital Sales & Revenue Analytics

Write-up in progress

An insurance business selling through digital platforms needed a reliable view of how those channels were performing. I built the transformation and data-quality layer in Databricks with PySpark, turning raw quote, policy, product and transaction data into analysis-ready datasets, then validated the output against the transaction system.

Problem
Limited visibility into product traction, policy sales and premium generated through digital platforms.
Approach
Standardised inconsistent text fields so keys and categories matched, joined the datasets, filtered and classified records, aggregated to reporting level, and ran data-quality checks.
Validation
Transformed outputs reconciled against transaction-system figures.

Practice

→Reconcile before you publish. A number the business can't tie back to a system it trusts doesn't get used.
→Standardise keys before joining. Most "wrong" numbers start as a trailing space.
→Build quality checks into the pipeline, not into a follow-up email.
→Separate what I did from what the team did. Credit is part of accuracy.
On confidentiality. All professional work shown here is anonymised. No company, customer or employee data, no policy records, no internal systems or proprietary code. Where figures would identify the business, they are described rather than quoted.
